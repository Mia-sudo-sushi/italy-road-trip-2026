30 Sep – 3 Oct · choose by weather

Dolomites weather window

Order decided 2–3 days before based on weather. All routes start from Canazei; the three plans are not assigned rigidly to dates.

30 Sep–3 Oct is our flexible Dolomites weather window. We aim to complete the three main mountain plans by 2 Oct where practical. 30 Sep is primarily Venice plus transfer / arrival, with only light nearby sightseeing if arrival is early and weather excellent. One plan may move to 3 Oct if weather requires; the three full plans are not guaranteed to fit. The 22:30 rental-car return at Milan Linate remains the hard deadline.

For 3 Oct, prioritize early-finishing western / Canazei-side activities: Seceda, Alpe di Siusi, Passo Pordoi or nearby scenic routes. Do not schedule Tre Cime full loop on 3 Oct. Check the drive from the chosen finish point and work backwards from 22:30, allowing for traffic, rest stops and the return process. Reduce or skip Milan sightseeing that day; 4 Oct is the main Milan sightseeing day.

Plan A · Seceda & Alpe di SiusiRidgeline views, meadows and huts · Highest weather priority
Buy on the day

If moved to 3 Oct, shorten this plan to finish early enough for the drive to Linate; the full schedule below is for a normal mountain day.

  1. Canazei → Ortisei. Aim to arrive around 08:00–08:15.
  2. 08:30 planned cable car: Ortisei → Furnes → Seceda.
  3. Approx. 09:00–11:30: famous ridgeline viewpoint, easy panoramic walk, photos and an optional mountain hut snack or coffee.
  4. Approx. 12:00 cable car down. Lunch in the Seceda / Ortisei area.
  5. Approx. 13:00 walk to the Ortisei–Alpe di Siusi gondola. Up around 13:15–13:30.
  6. 13:45–16:30: Alpe di Siusi easy walking, meadows, huts and Sassolungo views.
  7. Approx. 17:00 gondola down, then return to Canazei.

Plan C · Lago di Braies & scenic mountain passesPasso Giau, Arabba and Passo Pordoi · Most weather-flexible

Morning: Canazei → Lago di Braies / Pragser Wildsee. Spend approx. 1–1.5 hours around the lake: a scenic stop, not another major hike.

The scenic loop

Canazei → Lago di Braies → Cortina area → Passo Falzarego → Passo Giau → Arabba → Passo Pordoi → Canazei.

  • Core: Lago di Braies, Passo Giau, Passo Pordoi and scenic mountain passes.
  • Passo Falzarego: possible stop.
  • Passo Giau: major stop.
  • Arabba: coffee / food.
  • Passo Pordoi: final major pass before Canazei.

Optional · Buy on the day Sass Pordoi cable car: only if visibility is excellent. Skip if clouded in.

Costs: mountain pass roads and Lago di Braies free; lake parking, fuel and optional cable car extra. Nothing essential to book at this stage.

This is the most flexible plan, not an all-weather route. Adjust for road conditions and visibility. For a 3 Oct backup, choose Passo Pordoi or nearby Canazei-side scenic routes instead of the full eastern lake-and-passes loop, finishing early enough for the drive to Linate.

Before setting out

Practical notes

  • ZTL & parking: check permitted access before entering historic centres. Plan a legal car park; don’t follow navigation blindly into a restricted zone. Padova hotel parking is €6/day.
  • Toll roads: allow for tolls; choose a lane suitable for your payment method and rental setup.
  • Mountain driving: passes, traffic and stops can extend estimates. On 3 Oct work backwards from the 22:30 LIN return, with a generous buffer.
  • Weather & lifts: decide the order 2–3 days ahead, then recheck conditions and last lift times on the day. Carry warm layers and waterproof / windproof clothing.
  • Tre Cime: online vehicle access reservation required in the current plan; check the operator’s number-plate and access rules. Never assign the full loop to 3 Oct.
  • Airport buffer: return the car at Linate on 3 Oct; fly from Malpensa on 4 Oct. Leave generous time for the transfer, baggage and security.
