Transfer-day constraint: arrival is 30 Sep after Venice, leaving two full mountain days plus 3 Oct’s Milan transfer day. The three full plans below are options, not a promise that all fit. Shorten the plan on 3 Oct and set a departure time with ample driving and return buffer for Linate at 22:30.
Day A · Seceda & Alpe di SiusiRidgeline views, meadows and huts · Highest weather priority
Buy on the day
  1. Canazei → Ortisei. Aim to arrive around 08:00–08:15.
  2. 08:30 planned cable car: Ortisei → Furnes → Seceda.
  3. Approx. 09:00–11:30: famous ridgeline viewpoint, easy panoramic walk, photos and an optional mountain hut snack or coffee.
  4. Approx. 12:00 cable car down. Lunch in the Seceda / Ortisei area.
  5. Approx. 13:00 walk to the Ortisei–Alpe di Siusi gondola. Up around 13:15–13:30.
  6. 13:45–16:30: Alpe di Siusi easy walking, meadows, huts and Sassolungo views.
  7. Approx. 17:00 gondola down, then return to Canazei.

Food options

Sofie Hut — priority · Troier Hütte — alternative · Restaurant Seceda — easy backup · Restaurant Tubladel — proper dinner in Ortisei.

Costs & tickets

Seceda return €74 pp + Alpe di Siusi return €41 pp = approx. €460 for 4 adults. Gardena Card: €124 pp / €496 for 4. Do not buy yet: individual tickets in this plan are €36 cheaper overall.

Advance booking is not essential in the current plan; buy on the day to preserve weather flexibility. Times are planning targets; check seasonal lift operations and last descents before setting out.

Day B · Tre Cime full loop + Lago di MisurinaThe earliest and longest day · Very high weather priority
Book in advance

Leave Canazei approx. 06:00–06:30. Drive via Misurina to Rifugio Auronzo.

Full loop · allow approx. 4–5 hours

Rifugio Auronzo → Rifugio Lavaredo → Forcella Lavaredo → Locatelli / Dreizinnenhütte area → loop back to Auronzo.

Bring water, sandwiches / snacks, hiking shoes, a warm layer and a waterproof / windproof jacket.

Tre Cime vehicle access: €40 per car. ONLINE RESERVATION REQUIRED. Vehicle registration can be added by 23:59 the day before entry. This is the most important advance booking item in the current plan.

After the hike

Lago di Misurina · 45–90 minute scenic stop.

Food: Quinz Locanda al Lago for late lunch / early dinner · Malga Misurina · Pizzeria Edelweiss · Rifugio Auronzo for convenient hiking-day food / refreshments.

Optional Lago di Dobbiaco / Toblacher See, only if time and energy allow. Skip if the Tre Cime hike runs long — no need to turn today into a checklist.

Costs: hike free; vehicle access / parking €40 per car; Misurina free; Dobbiaco free if added. Other food and parking extra.

Day C · Lago di Braies & scenic mountain passesPasso Giau, Arabba and Passo Pordoi · Most weather-flexible

Morning: Canazei → Lago di Braies / Pragser Wildsee. Spend approx. 1–1.5 hours around the lake: a scenic stop, not another major hike.

The scenic loop

Canazei → Lago di Braies → Cortina area → Passo Falzarego → Passo Giau → Arabba → Passo Pordoi → Canazei.

  • Core: Lago di Braies, Passo Giau, Passo Pordoi and scenic mountain passes.
  • Passo Falzarego: possible stop.
  • Passo Giau: major stop.
  • Arabba: coffee / food.
  • Passo Pordoi: final major pass before Canazei.

Optional · Buy on the day Sass Pordoi cable car: only if visibility is excellent. Skip if clouded in.

Costs: mountain pass roads and Lago di Braies free; lake parking, fuel and optional cable car extra. Nothing essential to book at this stage.

This is the most flexible plan, not an all-weather route. Adjust for road conditions and visibility. On 3 Oct, shorten or reroute it for the drive to Milan instead of assuming the full loop fits.

Mountain priorities

Must / highest: Seceda · Tre Cime full loop · Lago di Braies · Passo Giau.

Included: Alpe di Siusi · Lago di Misurina · Passo Pordoi / scenic passes.

Optional only: Lago di Dobbiaco. Removed: Lago di Sorapis.
